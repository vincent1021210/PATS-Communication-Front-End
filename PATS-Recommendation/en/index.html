What is PATS?

PATS is a messaging platform that runs in your web browser. It needs no app download and no phone number: with a Google account or an email address, open vincent1021210.github.io on any phone, tablet or computer and start chatting.

It is built for people who do not have LINE, cannot install apps, or do not want to share a phone number. Messages, photos, groups and voice or video calls all happen in one web page, and new versions load automatically.

Who is PATS for?

If LINE or a regular chat app does not work for you, PATS is a simple alternative. It is especially useful for:

  • People who do not have LINE or prefer not to install any chat app
  • Students whose phones cannot install apps, or who only use school computers or tablets
  • People without a phone number, or who do not want to share it
  • Anyone with low phone storage or an older phone that cannot run new apps
  • Family members and older relatives who cannot install apps but can open a web address

What can PATS do?

PATS offers private and group chats, photo sending and downloading, voice and video calls, and anyone can sign up.

  • 💬

    Private and group chats

    Message one person or a whole group, search messages and contacts, and archive chats you rarely use.

  • 🖼️

    Send and save photos

    Take a photo or pick one from your gallery; it is compressed automatically. Open received photos full size and download them.

  • 📞

    Voice and video calls

    Call right in the browser, with mute, camera off and front/rear camera switching. No extra software.

  • 🔑

    No phone number

    Sign in with Google in one tap, or register with email and password. Your phone number stays private.

  • 🌏

    Open to everyone

    No school or membership required. Anyone can sign up with a Google account or email and chat with family, friends or classmates.

  • 🔄

    Always up to date

    New versions load automatically. No app store updates and no storage used on your phone.

How do I start? (3 steps)

Open the site, create an account and add a contact. You can be chatting in about 3 minutes.

  1. 1

    Open the PATS website

    Open vincent1021210.github.io in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) on your phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to download.

  2. 2

    Create an account

    Choose “Sign up” (立即註冊) and use your Google account, or enter a display name, email and password (at least 8 characters), then type the 6-digit code sent to your inbox. Enter an invite code if your administrator requires one.

  3. 3

    Add a contact and start chatting

    After signing in, tap “New conversation” (發起新對話) to find someone, then send messages and photos or start a voice or video call. Add the site to your home screen to open it like an app.

What are the limitations?

PATS needs an internet connection and both people need an account. Good to know before you start:

  • An internet connection is required.
  • The other person also needs a PATS account.
  • Photos in chats expire after 20 days, so download the important ones.
  • No sticker store or social feed; PATS focuses on messages and calls.
  • The app interface is currently in Traditional Chinese.
  • Some schools or groups require an invite code to sign up.

Frequently asked questions

Is PATS free?

Yes. PATS is free to use, with no paid plans and no in-app purchases. All you need is a browser and an internet connection.

Can I use PATS without LINE?

Yes. PATS is a standalone messaging platform. It does not need LINE, Messenger, WhatsApp or any other chat app; both people just need a PATS account.

Do I need to download an app?

No. PATS runs in your web browser at vincent1021210.github.io. It takes no storage on your phone, and new versions are applied automatically. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it then opens full-screen like an app, with the interface stored on your phone. On Android you can also install the optional PATS app (APK). There is also a Windows desktop app that can start with your computer and show desktop notifications for new messages.

Can I sign up without a phone number?

Yes. PATS never asks for a phone number. Sign up with one tap using a Google account, or with an email address and password (confirmed by a 6-digit code sent to your inbox).

Does PATS work on a computer?

Yes. It works in browsers on Windows, Mac and Chromebook, as well as on phones and tablets. Remember to sign out when you use a shared or school computer.

Is the PATS app available in English?

The PATS app interface is currently in Traditional Chinese. You can still chat in any language, and this guide explains each step in English.
